# GOAL 25 Independent Validation

No eligible external independent corpus was available locally for this evaluation. The GOAL21, GOAL23, GOAL24, and post-slice fixtures are useful controlled evidence, but they are not evidence of external independent generalization.

The A/B sample deliberately includes both positive and negative cases from these distinct internal sources. That reduces one-sided sampling risk, but it does not establish an independent validation claim. No corpus, oracle, hash, or historical benchmark was changed to improve results.

Because the NVIDIA arm is unverified, this run supplies no live-provider independent-validation metric. An external frozen corpus with independently maintained labels is required before an RC1 decision can rely on generalization evidence.
